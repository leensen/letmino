PRIVACY POLICY — LETMINO MILEAGE

Last updated: 05/09/2026

Letmino Mileage is designed around one principle: your data stays yours.

WHAT DATA DOES THE APP PROCESS?
- Odometer readings, photos of your dashboard, GPS locations and trip data
  that you record yourself.
- Settings such as your name, employer, rates, language, distance unit and
  currency.

WHERE IS THIS DATA STORED?
Only locally on your device. The app has no account, no server and no cloud
storage. We (Letmino) have no access to your data and collect nothing.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE?
- Nothing automatically. Exports (Excel, CSV, backup) leave your device only
  when you share them yourself through the Android share sheet.
- The app downloads a text-recognition model and fonts once, and uses the
  Android Geocoder (Google Play services) to turn GPS coordinates into a
  street name. No personal data is provided to us in the process; Google's
  privacy policy applies to these services. Without an internet connection
  the reading is saved with coordinates only and you can type the location.

DELETING DATA
Delete photos of finished periods in Settings → Storage, replace data with
the import function, or uninstall the app. Because everything is local, you
are the only administrator.
